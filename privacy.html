Beta Terms & Privacy Notice

Tennis Stat Tracker beta · Version 2026-10-beta3 · Effective October 12, 2026

1. What this is

Tennis Stat Tracker is in a free, early beta, open to people worldwide. Features can change, break or be removed, and beta cloud data may be reset when the full version launches. We'll give notice and a chance to export first.

Use at your own risk. The beta is provided "as is" and "as available", without warranties of any kind. To the fullest extent the law where you live allows, we are not liable for lost data, interrupted service, or decisions made using the app's stats. Keep your own copies of anything important. Some places don't allow these limits, so they apply only as far as local law permits.

2. Who can use it

  • Accounts are for adults only: 18 or older, or the age of majority where you live if that's higher. Parents and guardians create and manage the account for a junior player.
  • Children must not create accounts or use the app on their own.
  • If you record information about a minor who isn't your child, you confirm you have permission from their parent or legal guardian.

3. What we collect

  • Account: your email address and the role you choose (coach, player, parent).
  • Your tennis data, if you turn on cloud backup: a copy of the app's data on your device. That includes player names you enter, match scores and point-by-point stats, notes, assessments and settings. Videos stay on your device and are not uploaded.
  • Feedback you send us.
  • Technical data: error messages from the app, app version, and device type (for example "iPhone · Safari").

We don't use advertising, sell personal information, or share it for others' marketing.

4. Why we use it

  • To back up and restore your data across devices.
  • To fix bugs and improve the app, including reviewing beta usage and match data in aggregate.
  • To contact you about the beta (password-reset emails you request, important changes).

5. Where it's stored and who processes it

Data is stored with our cloud database provider (Supabase) and sign-in emails are sent through our email delivery provider. These providers process data only to run the service for us. Access to beta data is limited to the beta team.

The service is run from the United States. If you use it from another country, your data will be transferred to and stored in the United States.

6. How long we keep it

  • Cloud backups: the 10 most recent plus one per day for 14 days, up to 50 MB per account; older backups are deleted automatically.
  • Feedback and error reports: until the beta ends, then deleted or anonymized within 90 days.
  • Everything: deleted when you delete your account (see below).

7. Your choices

  • Delete everything: in the app, tap ☁ then Delete my cloud account & data. This removes your cloud data and login immediately. Data on your phone stays until you clear it.
  • Use it without the cloud: the app works fully offline without signing in. Nothing is uploaded unless you sign in.
  • Questions, access or correction requests: email dc@brinsonenterprise.com.
  • Parents and guardians: if you think we hold information about your child without your permission, email us and we'll delete it.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Each account can only read its own cloud data, enforced by the database. No system is perfectly secure; please don't put information in notes that you wouldn't want stored, such as medical details or home addresses.

9. Changes

If we change these terms, we'll update the version and date above and email beta testers before the change takes effect.
