Tennis Stat Tracker

Chart matches point by point on your phone, even with no signal, and get serve, return, momentum and pressure stats right after the match.

Open the app

Install it in 30 seconds

  1. Open this page in Safari. Other iPhone browsers can't install apps.
  2. Tap Open the app.
  3. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Always open it from the new home-screen icon. That's what keeps your data safe and lets it work offline.
  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap Open the app.
  3. Tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  4. Open it from the new icon from now on.
  1. The app is built for phones at courtside, but it also runs in Chrome, Edge or Safari on a computer.
  2. Click Open the app. In Chrome or Edge you can install it with the install icon in the address bar.

Then do this

1 · Turn on backupTap the ☁ button (bottom-left) and create a free account with your email and a password.
2 · Track 2 matchesTap Track match. Start with Basic tracking, then try Standard.
3 · Tell usTap ☁ → Send feedback. What was confusing, slow or missing?

It works offline. Your data saves on your phone and backs up to the cloud when you're back online.

Your data

Accounts are for adults: parents and guardians, coaches and adult players. Parents set up and manage the account for a junior player. The beta is free, open worldwide, and used at your own risk. You can delete your cloud account and data at any time from the ☁ menu.
